Privacy Policy

Tutu Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Tutu and Lulu" to upload videos to that channel.

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and set their titles and descriptions. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of any other user. The access token is stored only as an encrypted secret of the owner's own automation and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
